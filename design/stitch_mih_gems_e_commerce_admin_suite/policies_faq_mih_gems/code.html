Shipping & Delivery

Domestic & International Couriers

We partner with premium, fully insured courier services to ensure the safe and discrete delivery of your MIH GEMS pieces globally. All shipments are trackable and require a signature upon receipt.

  • Domestic Expedited (1-3 Business Days): Complimentary
  • International Priority (3-7 Business Days): Standard rates apply at checkout
  • Bespoke/Custom Orders: Please allow an additional 2-4 weeks for craftsmanship before dispatch.

Insurance & Liability

Every piece is fully insured by MIH GEMS during transit until it is delivered to your specified address. Responsibility for the item passes to you upon signature of receipt.

Returns & Refunds

Our Commitment

We stand behind the exceptional quality and authenticity of every gemstone and jewelry piece. If you are not entirely satisfied, we offer a meticulous return process to ensure your peace of mind.

Conditions for Return

  • Items must be returned within 14 days of receipt.
  • Pieces must remain unworn, unaltered, and in their original pristine condition.
  • All original packaging, certificates (e.g., GIA), and documentation must be included.
  • Custom or bespoke creations are considered final sale and are not eligible for return.

Process

To initiate a return, please contact our concierge team. We will provide a fully insured return shipping label and detailed instructions. Upon receipt and inspection by our gemologists, refunds will be processed to the original method of payment within 5-7 business days.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2023

Discretion & Data Protection

MIH GEMS values the utmost discretion and privacy of our clientele. We are committed to safeguarding your personal information with the highest standards of security.

Information Collection

We collect information essential for fulfilling orders, providing personalized concierge services, and complying with international anti-money laundering (AML) regulations concerning high-value transactions. This includes name, billing/shipping addresses, payment details, and purchase history.

Data Usage & Sharing

Your data is strictly used for order processing and internal analytics. We do not sell or share your personal information with third-party marketers. Data may only be shared with verified partners (e.g., insured couriers) strictly necessary to fulfill our services to you.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are all gemstones certified? expand_more

Yes. All significant gemstones curated by MIH GEMS are accompanied by authenticity certificates from internationally recognized gemological laboratories, such as GIA, AGL, or SSEF.

Do you offer bespoke design services? expand_more

Absolutely. Our master craftsmen work closely with clients to create exceptional, one-of-a-kind pieces. Please contact our concierge to arrange a private consultation regarding custom jewelry.

How should I care for my jewelry? expand_more

We recommend storing pieces individually in the provided velvet-lined boxes. Avoid exposure to harsh chemicals, extreme temperatures, and abrasive surfaces. Professional cleaning and inspection are recommended annually.
